**[E-commerce Business Performance (Olist)](https://github.com/m-yesmukhanbet/ecommerce-end-to-end-analysis)** · SQL, Python, Tableau
98,666 orders and R$15.8M in payments on the Brazilian Olist marketplace: revenue structure by month, category and state, delivery reliability, seller geography.

**[Banking Customer Churn Analysis](https://github.com/m-yesmukhanbet/banking-customer-churn-analysis)** · Python, scikit-learn
10,000 retail-bank customers, 20.4% churn. EDA plus three models compared on recall; Gradient Boosting reaches 0.87 accuracy and 0.50 recall, and the README explains why recall is the metric that matters.
